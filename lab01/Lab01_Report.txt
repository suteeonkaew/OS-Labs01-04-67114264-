3. Machine-Learning Analysis

Knowing the RAM limit prevents a 500,000-image dataset from being loaded all at once when it is larger than memory. Otherwise, the system may swap heavily, become very slow, or terminate the process because of insufficient memory. Knowing the CPU-core count also helps choose suitable worker and process counts for preprocessing and data loading. This makes the pipeline faster while avoiding excessive processes, memory use, and scheduling overhead.
